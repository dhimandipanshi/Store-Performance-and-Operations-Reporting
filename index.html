02Store & Regional Operations Funnel

KPI lifecycle coverage: Ingestion → KPI Calculation → Process Audit → Report Publishing

Report publishing completion trails ingestion by 3–8 points across regions — the funnel narrows most between KPI Calculation and Process Audit, not at the final publishing step.

Stage completion rate (%) at each lifecycle step, filtered by current selection above.

Ontario and British Columbia stay above 94% through Report Publishing; Quebec drops below 92% and Prairies trails close behind at 92.6%, both pointing to a KPI-Calculation-to-Audit bottleneck.

03Productivity & Process Efficiency

The core view — linking reconciliation speed to store productivity, region by region

Prairies and Quebec are the only regions to lose productivity quarter-over-quarter — both sorted first as the underperformers.

Y-axis: Store KPI Productivity Rate (%) · X-axis: Region, sorted by variance ascending (worst first)

Regions with rising reconciliation time (right chart) are the same regions losing productivity here — Prairies and Quebec.

AVG(fact.actual_value) WHERE dim_kpi.kpi_name='Store Productivity Rate' AND dim_scenerio='Actual', GROUP BY dim_region.region_name, dim_date.fiscal_quarter; variance = last_2Q − prior_2Q.

Reconciliation time is climbing in Quebec and Prairies — the two regions now missing their productivity target.

Y-axis: Median hours to resolve a data/reporting exception · X-axis: Month, one line per region

Ontario and BC cut resolution time by roughly 1.5–2 hrs since April; Quebec and Prairies are trending the opposite direction, each up about 3 hrs.

PERCENTILE_CONT(TIMESTAMP_DIFF(fact.resolved_at, fact.exception_opened_at, HOUR), 0.5) WHERE dim_activity_type.activity_type='Exception Reconciliation', GROUP BY dim_region.region_name, dim_date.month.

04Reporting & Source System Integration

Upstream pipeline accuracy vs. volume, and report utilization, by source system

Legacy Store Ops DB and the RPA Bot Farm carry the lowest pipeline accuracy despite processing the least volume — the two sources most worth auditing first.

Left axis: Data Pipeline Accuracy (%) — bars · Right axis: Records Processed (thousands) — line, by dim_source_sys

High-volume systems (SAP BW, Oracle Retail DW) stay above 97% accuracy; low-volume systems (Legacy Store Ops DB, RPA Bot Farm) dip to 91–94%.

accuracy = 1 − (COUNTIF(fact.record_status='Error') / COUNT(fact.record_id)); volume = COUNT(fact.record_id), both GROUP BY dim_source_sys.system_name, joined via fact_retail_reporting.source_sys_id.

The Daily Store Scorecard drives most report traffic — Exception Reconciliation Logs are opened least, despite feeding the productivity story above.

Report opens (count) by dim_report.report_name, current period

Increasing visibility of the Exception Reconciliation Log could shorten the resolution-time trend seen in View 3.

05Automation & Audit Readiness

RPA / Power Automate jobs completed before store opening, by department, vs. the 98% benchmark

Footwear and Beauty are the only departments missing the 98% pre-open automation benchmark.

Y-axis: % of automated reporting jobs completed successfully before daily store opening · X-axis: Department (dim_dept) · Dashed line: 98% readiness benchmark
